[Adblock Plus 2.0]
! Title: Hide Twitch Extensions
! Description: Turns Twitch channel extensions off for viewers (overlay, component and panel). Extensions only - no ads, no channel points, no chat, nothing else on Twitch is touched.
! Version: 2026.08.30.1
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/Dryader/hide-twitch-extensions
! License: https://github.com/Dryader/hide-twitch-extensions/blob/main/LICENSE
!
! Twitch gives viewers no switch for this: player extensions can be
! minimised, not disabled. A content blocker is the only viewer-side option.
!
! ============================================================
! 1. NETWORK RULE - the durable part of this list
! ============================================================
! Every Twitch extension renders inside an iframe served from
! <extension-id>.ext-twitch.tv, orchestrated by the parent frame at
! supervisor.ext-twitch.tv/supervisor/v1/index.html.
!
! Blocking that subdocument means extensions never load, rather than
! loading and being painted over. It matches on hostname, so Twitch
! front-end rewrites and hashed CSS class names cannot break it.
!
! Verified 2026-08-30: supervisor.ext-twitch.tv/supervisor/v1/index.html
! still returns HTTP 200 text/html.
||ext-twitch.tv^$subdocument
!
! ============================================================
! 2. COSMETIC CLEANUP - removes the leftover containers
! ============================================================
! With the frames blocked, Twitch still renders the wrappers: the
! extensions dock, the taskbar strip of extension buttons, and the
! overlay sizing box. Left in place they reserve empty space, and the
! overlay box is a known cause of the player controls becoming
! unclickable and the mouse cursor refusing to auto-hide over the video.
twitch.tv##[class*="extensions-dock"]
twitch.tv##.extensions-video-overlay-size-container
twitch.tv##.extension-taskbar
twitch.tv##.extension-taskbar-card-container
twitch.tv##.extension-view
twitch.tv##.extension-frame-wrapper
twitch.tv##.extensions-notifications
!
! ============================================================
! 3. OPTIONAL - panel extensions below the video
! ============================================================
! Rule 1 already stops panel extensions loading. Uncomment the line
! below only if a blocked panel extension leaves a visible gap under
! the stream. Procedural (:has) syntax, uBlock Origin / AdGuard only.
! twitch.tv#?#.channel-panels-container > div:has(a[href^="/ext/"])
!
! ============================================================
! NOT in this list, on purpose
! ============================================================
! - Ads. This is not an ad blocker.
! - Channel points, bits, share button, Prime offers, the front page
!   carousel, the GDPR banner. The one other filter list that hides
!   extensions (ultramegatom/adblock-twitch-garbage, last touched
!   2018) also strips all of those. This list does not.
! - Chat, badges, emotes, the leaderboard header in chat.
